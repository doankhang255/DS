Project introduction
1 · Introduction

FinNews Tracker: follow what the news says about each listed company, day by day

Why this project

  • Hundreds of company news articles appear every day across many sites — too many to read.
  • One article can be good for one company and bad for another: “VNM grows strongly while MSN reports a loss”.
  • Goal: label every news article per (article, company) as positive / negative / neutral.

What it shows

  • Pick a ticker + date range + news source → number of positive / negative / neutral articles.
  • News sentiment over time (chart) and the article list with links to the original.
  • Home page: tickers with the most positive news in the last 7 / 30 / 90 days.
  • Updated automatically every day.

Benefits

  • Investors: see the news picture of a company in seconds instead of reading every article.
  • Research: a clean, company-linked Vietnamese financial news dataset from 2010.
  • Not investment advice — a reference tool alongside prices and fundamentals.

Daily incremental crawl
3 · Data collection

After the history is collected, the crawler only fetches new articles each day

Resumable

Each finished (channel, month) is recorded and skipped next time; the current month is always re-listed to catch new articles.

No duplicates

Deduplicated by link; articles already downloaded are not fetched again; permanent errors (404) are logged and skipped.

Polite crawling

Minimum delay between requests, automatic retry on network errors, a log for every run.

7
Cleaning & duplicate merging
4 · Data preparation

Normalise each article, then merge articles from different sites about the same event

Per-article cleaning

  • Normalise domain (drop https://, www.) and publish date (drop “(GMT+7)”)
  • Remove junk characters and extra whitespace; parse keywords
  • Cut trailing blocks such as “Có thể bạn quan tâm” (related articles)
  • Drop advertisements (e.g. course promotions)
  • Drop duplicate links

Cross-site duplicates

Sites rarely publish the same article, but often cover the same event:

vietstock“Hòa Phát lãi hơn 8.2 ngàn tỷ trong quý 3”
CafeF“Hòa Phát lãi 8.265 tỷ đồng, tăng 106% trong quý 3/2026”
Rule: same subject company, published ≤ 48 hours apart, same key facts (key figures or text similarity) → same dup_group_id, counted as one event; the earliest article represents the group.
